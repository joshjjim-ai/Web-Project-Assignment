Getting to Cougar Commons

Pick the way that fits your day.

Driving and parking

Student parking is available in the lot beside the building. Arrive before 9 AM on weekdays for the best chance at a nearby space.

Campus shuttle

UH shuttles runs every hour on weekdays between the main lot and the front entrance. For More information please go to https://www.uh.edu/parking/cougar-line/

Walking

A lit walking path connects to the residence area.

Rideshare and drop-off

Use the marked drop-off loop at the front entrance. It is short-stop only, so please do not park there.

Find us on the map

Map is centered on the Sugar Land campus area. Adjust the bbox values in the iframe URL to move it.
