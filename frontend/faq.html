Frequently asked questions

Select a question to see the answer.

Who can use Cougar Commons?

Any UH student, faculty, or staff member with a valid Cougar Card. Guests are welcome when they arrive with a cardholder.

Do I need a reservation to study in the library?

No. Library seating is first come, first served. Only the five study rooms need a booking.

How long can I keep a study room?

Up to three hours per booking, and up to fourteen days ahead. Rooms are released if nobody arrives within fifteen minutes.

Can I bring food and drinks into the library?

Drinks with lids are fine anywhere. Meals stay in the café and the atrium seating area.

Is printing available?

Yes. Two printing stations sit on the library's first floor. Charge prints to your Cougar Card.
