The library

Two floors of quiet seating, shared tables, and power outlets at almost every seat. Bring a laptop and stay a while.

More about the library

Borrow textbooks, use the printing station, or ask the front desk for help finding a source.

The café

Coffee, breakfast tacos, and grab-and-go lunches between classes. Seating spills into the library atrium.

More about the café

Vegetarian and gluten-free options are on the menu every day, and you can pay with your Cougar Card.
